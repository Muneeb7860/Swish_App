# Swish OS: GM Business & EBITDA Strategy
**Author**: General Manager  
**Audience**: VC Investors / Board of Directors  

---

## 🎯 1. The Test Flight Niche Market
*   **Target Segment**: **Retail Convenience & Perishable Food Chains** (Zurich/Geneva transport hubs).
*   **Why this Niche**: 
    - Fresh food logistics require high-accuracy cold-chain thermal invariant tracking and SLA speed controls.
    - Our existing **IoT Telemetry, Spoilage Alarm, and Ledgers** match this sector's auditing and compliance needs.
    - Entry barriers are lower compared to high-cost clinical pharmaceutical validation (GDP), which is deferred.
*   **Pilot Partner**: **Valora (k kiosk)**. We integrate with 5 high-traffic transport hub stores to manage replenishment of temperature-sensitive fresh retail foods and perishables.

---

## 💡 2. The Value Proposition (What we Bring)
1.  **Zero-Leak Contract Procurement**: AI agents negotiate optimal terms with wholesalers based on volume discounts, saving on average **3% to 7%** in procurement margins.
2.  **Instant Cold-Chain Audit Compliance**: Real-time telemetry is cryptographically locked into the ledger. This guarantees compliance reports for regulators in one click.
3.  **Automated Exception Handling**: The WMS manages tasks autonomously, only alerting human operators when exceptions are triggered.

---

## 📈 3. The Path to EBITDA Positive (Financial Roadmap)

```
[Year 1] ➔ Direct Pilot validation (5 hubs, EBITDA: Neutral)
   │
   ▼
[Year 2] ➔ SaaS Rollout to 100 hubs ($1,500/month/hub Tier 2 subscription, EBITDA: +$240K)
   │
   ▼
[Year 3] ➔ Enterprise network scaling (500+ stores, EBITDA: +$2.2M)
```

*   **Year 1 (Prove & Retain)**: Establish reference cases with 5 pilot hubs. Charge Tier 1 entry pricing ($1,000/month) to secure logo credibility. EBITDA: Neutral.
*   **Year 2 (Expand & Scale)**: Upgrade stores to Tier 2 subscription ($1,500/month/hub) for automated agentic replenishment. Scale to 100 active stores across Switzerland. EBITDA turns positive at **+$240K** without complex commission audits.
*   **Year 3 (Network Dominance)**: Scale to 500+ stores globally via SaaS partners. EBITDA scales to **+$2.2M** from recurring software licenses and zero operational overhead.
