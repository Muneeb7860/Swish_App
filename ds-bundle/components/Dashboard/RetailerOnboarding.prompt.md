# RetailerOnboarding

Multi-step form flow for onboarding new retailers into the platform.

## Features
- Step-by-step progression
- Form validation and submission
- Success states and confirmations
- Business information collection
- Compliance/agreement acceptance

## Use Cases
- New seller registration
- Business setup flow
- Account activation
- KYC workflows

## Composition
Combines form inputs, buttons, and progress indicators into a complete onboarding UX.
